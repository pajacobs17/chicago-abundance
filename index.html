Serving the Elmwood neighborhood since 1989

Neighbors taking care of neighbors.

We run the food pantry, the after-school tutoring room, and the community garden two blocks from here — all of it kept going by people who live on these same streets.

A place that's run by the people who use it.

Elmwood Community Coalition started at a kitchen table in 1989, when four neighbors pooled canned goods for families hit hard by a factory closing. We're still doing the same basic thing: noticing what our neighbors need and figuring out how to get it to them, together.

"This isn't charity from the outside. It's us, taking care of us." — Marisol Ortega, founding member

What we do

  • Food pantry & hot meals

    Open three mornings a week, no paperwork, no questions. We also cook a shared dinner every Thursday for anyone who wants company along with the meal.

  • Youth tutoring & mentorship

    Homework help and reading support for kids in grades 1 through 8, paired with a consistent adult mentor who sticks around for more than one semester.

  • Senior companion visits

    Weekly check-ins, grocery runs, and rides to appointments for older neighbors who live alone and want to stay in their own homes.

  • The Elm Street garden

    Forty raised beds on the old lot at Elm and 4th. Plots are free to any household in the neighborhood — we just ask that you show up and tend it.

Volunteer

We need hands most on pantry mornings, tutoring afternoons, and the first Saturday of every month in the garden. No experience needed — just show up once and we'll take it from there.
