Chicago Abundance PAC — an independent political action committee dedicated to supporting moderate abundant candidates in Chicago. We support an agenda which pushes back on the influence of the left through reducing housing costs, strengthening property rights, and improving government for all.

A New Approach.

Chicago Abundance PAC was formed by Chicagoans concerned by the relentless advance of extremeness in our politics. We seek to right the ship in Chicago by promoting sane politics around taxes, spending, and property rights.

Where we put our resources

  • Voter registration & turnout

    Door-to-door canvassing, registration drives at community events, and free rides to the polls, concentrated in precincts with historically low turnout.

  • Candidate support

    We endorse and fund candidates who agree with us and seek to advance our priorities

Volunteer

Canvassing, phone and text banking, and poll-worker rides. Training takes twenty minutes and we'll pair you with a team lead in your area.
