Our mission Priorities

The issues we're organizing around.

  • High Quality Education

    We believe that the job of our school system is to educate the next generation, not to be an ideological project for extremists. We seek to return CPS to being laser focused on students and student outcomes, not as a jobs program or political slush fund.

  • Cost of Living

    The relentless march of housing costs and everyday essentials has driven many people to radical left wing solutions. We see alleviating those costs as key to the pushback against extremist left-wing voices. This requires allowing property owners to exercise their rights unimpeded by the government, streamlining government, and reducing the burden of beauracratic processes.

  • Budgeting

    Our city continues to spend more than it takes in, year after year. Our politicians focus needs to be on generating a government surplus and structural fixes to Chicago's budget problems. No more nickel and diming to get through each budget cycle.

  • Efficient Government

    The job of the government is to set rules and get out of the way. In far too many cases, our government is slow, inefficient, and outdated. Bring back a focus on quality and cost focused services delivered to citizens in their day to day lives.
